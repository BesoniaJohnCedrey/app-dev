### Wuthering Waves

## Wuthering Waves is a free-to-play game released in 2024 by Kuro Games. We take control of Rover, whose memories have been missing from before the start of the game. We follow his story while trying to save the world of Solaris-3 from the effects of the lament. We come across an array of characters, enjoyable scenes, and emotional ones as we uncover the truth about the one we play as.
